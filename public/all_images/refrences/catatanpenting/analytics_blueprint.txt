============================================
ANALYTICS PAGE - BLUEPRINT
Disimpan: 16 Mei 2026
============================================

## STRUKTUR TAB UTAMA (3 tab di atas)

1. ACTIVITY RECAP
2. HABIT PATTERN
3. STATS (RPG)

Masing-masing section punya toggle sendiri: Weekly | Monthly | 90 Days

============================================

## TAB 1: ACTIVITY RECAP

Isi:
- Dot calendar (hari aktif = hijau, miss = abu)
- Summary:
  - Habit completion rate (total habit x hari / yang selesai = %)
    Contoh: 5 habit x 7 hari = 35 total. Selesai 10 = 28.5%
  - To-Do yang dikerjakan (berapa todo selesai dari total)
  - Journey entries (nulis jurnal atau ga)
- Toggle: Weekly | Monthly | 90 Days (masing-masing nunjukin data sesuai range)

Logic:
- Day 1 = hari pertama user download & mulai app (bukan fixed date)
- Day counter = selisih hari dari tanggal join sampai sekarang

============================================

## TAB 2: HABIT PATTERN

Isi:
- Per habit analysis:
  - Completion rate per habit
  - Hari paling sering bolong (misal "Sering miss di Minggu")
  - Streak per habit
  - Trend naik/turun
- Toggle: Weekly | Monthly | 90 Days

============================================

## TAB 3: STATS (RPG)

Isi:
- Radar chart (pentagon): Wisdom, Confidence, Strength, Discipline, Focus
- Level + XP bar
- Stat list dengan angka + arrow (hijau naik, merah turun)
- Toggle: Current | Day 1 | Day X (comparison)

Formula:
- XP = akumulasi (ga bisa turun). Setiap habit selesai = +XP
- Stats = refleksi performa SAAT INI (bisa naik turun)
  - Wisdom = journaling consistency
  - Confidence = streak terpanjang + total hari aktif
  - Strength = habit fisik completion rate
  - Discipline = overall completion rate
  - Focus = deep work / learning habits completion

Scaling:
- Agak susah naiknya, terasa puas di day 90
- Bisa berlangsung 1 year
- Proses ga lambat banget tapi meaningful

============================================

## CATATAN TEKNIS

- Day start = profile.created_at (tanggal join)
- Completion rate = (total habit selesai) / (total habit x jumlah hari) x 100
- Data source: habit_logs table di Supabase
- Untuk sekarang pake dummy data dulu
